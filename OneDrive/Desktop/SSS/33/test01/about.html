Sri Dev Suman Uttarakhand University

A State University shaping future leaders with quality education in the lap of the Himalayas.

Sri Dev Suman Uttarakhand University (SDSUV), established by the Government of Uttarakhand, is located in Badshahithaul, Tehri Garhwal. The university is committed to offering high-quality, affordable, and accessible education to students from diverse backgrounds.

  • Government-recognized State University (UGC Approved)
  • Modern NEP-based teaching structure
  • Beautiful Himalayan location offering a peaceful learning environment
  • Diverse range of UG, PG, Diploma & Professional courses
  • Experienced faculty and strong academic foundation

With a vision to empower youth through education and skill development, SDSUV continues to contribute significantly to the educational growth of Uttarakhand.

Sri Dev Suman Uttarakhand University

A State University shaping future leaders with quality education in the lap of the Himalayas.

Sri Dev Suman Uttarakhand University (SDSUV), established by the Government of Uttarakhand, is located in the peaceful and scenic region of Badshahithaul, Tehri Garhwal. The university is committed to providing accessible, affordable, and high-quality education to students across the state and beyond.

  • Government-recognized State University (UGC Approved)
  • Modern NEP-based academic structure
  • Peaceful Himalayan environment ideal for learning
  • Experienced faculty and strong academic support
  • Wide range of UG, PG, professional & vocational courses

SDSUV aims to empower students with strong knowledge, practical skills, and creative thinking. The university continues to grow as a center of educational excellence in Uttarakhand.
