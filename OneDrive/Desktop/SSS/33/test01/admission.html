Admission Process

Step-by-step guide to apply for admission

1

Register Online

Visit the admission portal and create your account with your email and phone number.

2

Fill Application

Complete the application form with all required personal and academic details.

3

Upload Documents

Submit all required documents in PDF format as per the specified requirements.

4

Pay Fee & Submit

Complete the application fee payment and submit your application form.

Required Documents

Documents you need to submit for admission

Educational Certificates

  • 10th and 12th standard mark sheets
  • Graduation degree certificate and mark sheet
  • Migration certificate
  • Character certificate

Identification & Personal Documents

  • Aadhar card copy
  • Passport size photograph (4x6 cm)
  • Birth certificate
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)

Address Proof

  • Passport or Aadhar card
  • Voter ID or Driving License
  • Electricity or Water Bill
  • Rent agreement (if applicable)

Additional Documents

  • Entrance exam scorecard (if applicable)
  • Sports/Cultural achievement certificates
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Gap affidavit (if applicable)

Admission Criteria

Eligibility and selection criteria for various programs

Bachelor's Degree (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com)

  • Eligibility: 12th Pass from recognized board
  • Age Limit: No age limit
  • Selection: Merit-based on 12th marks
  • Minimum %: 45% aggregate

Master's Degree (M.A., M.Sc., M.Com)

  • Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in relevant field
  • Age Limit: No age limit
  • Selection: Merit in Entrance Exam
  • Minimum %: 50% in graduation

Ph.D. Program

  • Eligibility: Master's degree in relevant field
  • Age Limit: No age limit
  • Selection: Interview & Research proposal
  • Minimum %: 55% in master's

Scholarships

Financial aid and scholarship opportunities available

Merit Scholarship

Up to 50%

  • Based on entrance exam score
  • Available for all programs
  • Renewable annually
  • Top 10% eligible students

Need-Based Scholarship

Up to 75%

  • For economically weaker students
  • Family income criteria apply
  • Requires financial documentation
  • Subject to university rules

Sports & Cultural Scholarship

Up to 100%

  • For national-level achievers
  • Sports and cultural activities
  • Waiver of tuition fees
  • Additional sports funding

Reserved Category Scholarship

Up to 100%

  • SC/ST/OBC students
  • Government norms apply
  • Full fee waiver eligible
  • Additional stipends available

Minority Scholarship

Up to 90%

  • For minority communities
  • As per central schemes
  • Partial fee assistance
  • Monthly stipend provided

Special Assistance Fund

Variable

  • For students in hardship
  • Emergency financial support
  • Application-based assistance
  • Processed quarterly
